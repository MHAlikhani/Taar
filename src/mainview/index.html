User Guide

  1. Launch the browser — click “Launch Browser”. An isolated Chrome window opens and your session is saved (log in once).
  2. Sign in to LinkedIn — in the browser window, log in, then go to linkedin.com/search/results/people (People Search).
  3. Configure — set how many requests to send (20–30 recommended for free accounts) and optionally write a personalized note.
  4. Start — press “Start”. The bot skips pending invitations automatically, paces itself like a human, and cools down when errors pile up.

Tips: if the browser window is closed, just press “Launch Browser” again — recovery is automatic. Your session is saved after every 5 invitations. Use “Stop” for a graceful stop.

Smart pacing: after repeated errors the bot slows down on its own to keep your account safe.
